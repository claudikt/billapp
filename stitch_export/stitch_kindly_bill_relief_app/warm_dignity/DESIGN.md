## Brand & Style

This design system serves a mobile-first mutual aid and micro-philanthropy platform dedicated to fostering human solidarity without pity. The aesthetic is warm editorial, drawing inspiration from modern literary layouts and human-centric matchmaking interfaces—thoughtful, structured, and emotionally resonant, yet strictly grounded and respectful.

### Emotional Tenets
- **Dignity over Charity:** Requestors and givers meet as equals. Interfaces avoid desperate visual tropes, pitying imagery, or sensationalist urgency.
- **Warmth with Rigor:** Grounded alabaster paper surfaces blend with deep forest greens and editorial typography to evoke a well-crafted journal or community ledger.
- **Radical Transparency & Safety:** Soft, deliberate micro-interactions, clear structural grouping, and dedicated verification cues instill enduring trust.

### Visual Style
A hybrid of **Warm Editorial** and **Tactile Elevation**:
- Generous internal padding and substantial structural corner radii ($24\text{px}$) impart an organic, human touch.
- Cream and soft alabaster paper backings provide warmth, avoiding the sterility of stark digital whites.
- Typography pairs an authoritative, human literary serif for storytelling titles with an ultra-clean, humanist sans-serif for functional UI and transactional clarity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional balance between natural grounding, editorial refinement, and heartfelt clarity:

- **Primary (`#16382C` Deep Forest Emerald):** Anchors primary brand touchpoints, dominant callouts, and foundational headers. Represents enduring stability, quiet stewardship, and depth.
- **Secondary (`#E06D53` Warm Coral / Amber):** Reserved strictly for primary action buttons (e.g., "Give Support", "Fulfill Need"), progress highlights, and warm accent moments. Conveys compassionate urgency without alarmist panic.
- **Tertiary (`#381E2F` Deep Plum):** Used for intimate narrative tags, secondary badges, and editorial quotes. Imparts warmth, craft, and distinct character.
- **Neutral (`#FAF8F5` Soft Warm Alabaster):** The default canvas surface. Paired with `#F4EFEB` for secondary card containers and muted inset wells.
- **Structural Border (`#D8E2DC` Muted Sage):** Defines hairline containment lines and subtle dividers with low contrast.
- **Verified Accent (`#0F766E` Deep Teal):** Exclusively utilized for identity validation, trust badges, and community vouch confirmations.
- **Text & Reading (`#1A201C` Deep Forest Charcoal):** Ensures AAA-compliant legibility across alabaster backgrounds while maintaining an organic, warm cast.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the literary authority of **Newsreader** with the approachable clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Roles & Expressive Usage
- **Newsreader (Headlines & Editorial Callouts):** Imparts dignity, narrative pacing, and weight. Headings must always maintain relaxed leading and slight negative tracking at large scales to avoid loose letter-spacing. Use italics sparingly for emphasis, personal quotes, or community pledges.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Functional UI, & Labels):** Provides crisp rendering on low-density mobile screens. Its open counters and geometric humanism ensure rapid comprehension across form inputs, donation breakdowns, and meta labels.
- **Scale Rules:** Display sizes greater than $24\text{px}$ automatically step down on mobile screens to preserve layout density and prevent single-word hyphenations.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid grid prioritizes focused single-column feeds and tactile story cards:

### Breakpoints & Layout Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column layout. Margin is set to `1.25rem` (20px), gutter to `1rem` (16px). Cards span the full safe width. Vertical rhythm relies on `space-md` for standard component stacking and `space-lg` between distinct editorial sections.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid. Margins expand to `2rem`, gutters to `1.5rem`. Request feeds adopt balanced 2-column masonry or modular side-by-side verification panels.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed grid capped at a maximum width of `1180px`. Left rail anchors navigation and community context; central 7-column corridor houses interactive cards, with a 3-column sticky support panel.

### Spatial Rhythm
Internal card padding never drops below `1.25rem` (20px) to give personal narratives adequate breathing room. Spacing scale tokens (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) define padding and element gaps exclusively.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical paper layering, soft warm glows, and low-contrast outlines rather than harsh drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Base Canvas (`#FAF8F5`):** The ambient warm alabaster foundation.
2. **Elevated Cards (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority focus units, story cards, and interactive forms resting directly on the canvas.
3. **Inset Wells (`#F4EFEB`):** Used inside cards to visually recede breakdown stats, breakdown calculations, and progress tracking meters.

### Ambient Shadow & Border Matrix
- **Low Elevation (Resting Cards):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(22, 56, 44, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(56, 30, 47, 0.05)`, encased by a `1px` border of `#D8E2DC`.
- **Medium Elevation (Active / Pressed Cards):** `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(22, 56, 44, 0.06), 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(224, 109, 83, 0.08)`, border transitioning to `#C6D4CC`.
- **High Elevation (Bottom Sheets & Modals):** `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(22, 56, 44, 0.16)`.
- **Warm Accent Glow:** Primary CTAs leverage a tinted ambient diffusion: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(220, 109, 83, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces generous, organic curves that feel approachable and crafted:

- **Cards & Modal Sheets:** Use a standard $24\text{px}$ (`rounded-xl` / $1.5\text{rem}$) radius to reinforce the soft tactile paper motif.
- **Interactive Inputs & Buttons:** Use $16\text{px}$ (`rounded-lg` / $1\text{rem}$) for large buttons and text containers, striking a balance between structure and welcoming curves.
- **Tags, Badges, & Avatars:** Fully rounded pill silhouettes ($9999\text{px}$) for trust badges, category pills, and micro-counters.
- **Dividers & Strokes:** Soft terminal ends on progress bars and micro-rules, maintaining fluidity across geometric boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Support / Give):** Filled with `#E06D53` Coral, text in `#FFFFFF` (`label-lg`), height $52\text{px}$, corner radius $16\text{px}$. Includes an ambient coral glow shadow. Hover/active shifts to `#CE5E45`.
- **Secondary (Editorial Action / Read More):** Background of `#16382C` Forest Emerald with `#FAF8F5` text, or transparent background with a $1.5\text{px}$ border of `#16382C` and `#16382C` text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Unbounded text button with underline accent, styled in `#381E2F` with minimal horizontal padding for secondary actions within cards.

### Cards (The Dignity Card)
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `#FAF8F5` canvas, bounded by a $1\text{px}$ border of `#D8E2DC` with a $24\text{px}$ radius and $20\text{px}$ ($1.25\text{rem}$) internal padding.
- **Header:** Author avatar ($44\text{px}$, rounded circle) with adjacent verification badge, author name in `headline-sm`, and category pill tag aligned top-right.
- **Narrative Zone:** Story snippet set in `body-md` (`#1A201C`) with generous line-height ($22\text{px}$), followed by key quote callouts set in `headline-md` italic.
- **Aid Progress / Direct Impact Ledger:** Inset well background (`#F4EFEB`, $16\text{px}$ radius, $12\text{px}$ padding) showing funding or fulfillment progress via a rounded coral bar on muted sage track.

### Chips & Badges
- **Verified Trust Badge:** Solid pill with `#0F766E` background, white text, and a crisp check icon (`label-sm`).
- **Category & Vouch Pills:** Translucent background (`rgba(22, 56, 44, 0.06)`), text in `#16382C`, border of `1px solid rgba(22, 56, 44, 0.12)`.
- **Direct Urgent Need Pill:** Subtle warm plum tint (`#381E2F` at 8% opacity), text in `#381E2F`, uppercase tracking.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Container:** Minimum height of $52\text{px}$, $16\text{px}$ radius, background `#FFFFFF`, border $1\text{px}$ `#D8E2DC`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#16382C` with a soft outer ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(22, 56, 44, 0.12)`.
- **Labels:** Positioned above the field in `label-md` with `#16382C` coloring. Helper text rendered in `body-sm` (`#5A6860`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox:** $22\text{px} \times 22\text{px}$ with a $6\text{px}$ radius. Default border is $1.5\text{px}$ `#D8E2DC`. Checked state fills with `#16382C` displaying a sharp alabaster checkmark.
- **Radio:** $22\text{px} \times 22\text{px}$ circle. Selected state shows a `#16382C` outer ring enclosing a `#16382C` center dot ($10\text{px}$) with an alabaster spacer gap.

### Specialized Component: Micro-Ledger Breakdown
- A dedicated itemized list detailing direct disbursement breakdown.
- Uses subtle dashed separators in `#D8E2DC`, currency amounts set in tabular `body-md` figures, and an accompanying verification seal to maintain full financial clarity.
